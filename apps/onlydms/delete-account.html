Account Deletion

How to delete your OnlyDMs account and what happens to your data

⚠️ Important Notice

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please read this page carefully before proceeding with account deletion.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your OnlyDMs account directly from the mobile app. Follow these simple steps:

1

Open the App

Launch the OnlyDMs app on your mobile device and make sure you're logged in.

2

Go to Profile

Tap on the "Profile" icon in the bottom navigation bar.

3

Access Settings

Tap the settings icon (⚙️) in the top-right corner of your profile page.

4

Find Delete Account

Scroll down to find the "Delete Account" option at the bottom of the settings page.

5

Confirm Deletion

Read the warning message carefully and confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.

6

Enter Password

For security purposes, you'll need to re-enter your password to confirm the deletion.

7

Final Confirmation

Tap "Delete My Account" to complete the process. Your account will be deleted immediately.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

🚨 Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

The following information will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered:

  • Profile Information: Your username, display name, bio, profile photo, and location
  • Account Details: Email address, authentication credentials, and account settings
  • Messages: All conversations, messages (text and audio), and media files you've sent or received
  • Contacts: Your connection list and conversation history with other users
  • Diamond Balance: Any remaining diamonds in your account (no refunds will be issued)
  • Purchase History: Records of diamond purchases and transactions
  • Call History: Video and voice call logs and records
  • Settings and Preferences: All app settings, notification preferences, and customizations

📋 What Happens Immediately

  • Your profile will be removed from search and browse results
  • Other users will no longer be able to message you
  • Your conversations will be deleted from your account and other users' accounts
  • You will be logged out of all devices
  • Your profile will become inaccessible

Important Considerations Before Deleting

💎 Diamond Purchases

Any remaining diamonds in your account will be lost upon deletion. No refunds will be issued for unused diamonds. We recommend using your diamonds before deleting your account.

💬 Active Conversations

All your conversations will be permanently deleted. If you have important messages, we recommend saving or backing them up before proceeding with account deletion.

📱 Subscription and Payments

If you have any active subscriptions or pending payments, please make sure to cancel them before deleting your account. Account deletion does not automatically cancel subscriptions through the Google Play Store.

⏰ Deletion Timeline

Account deletion is immediate, but some data may be retained for a limited time for the following reasons:

  • Legal Requirements: We may retain certain data for legal, tax, or regulatory purposes (typically 30-90 days)
  • Backup Systems: Data in backup systems will be deleted according to our backup retention schedule (within 30 days)
  • Fraud Prevention: Minimal data may be retained to prevent abuse and fraud

Alternative Options

💡 Consider These Alternatives

Before permanently deleting your account, you might want to consider:

  • Logout: Simply log out if you want to take a break without losing your data
  • Disable Notifications: Turn off push notifications in settings if they're bothering you
  • Clear Conversations: Delete individual conversations instead of your entire account
  • Update Privacy Settings: Adjust your profile visibility and privacy preferences

Can I Recover My Account?

⛔ No Account Recovery

Once your account is deleted, it CANNOT be recovered or restored. This includes:

  • Your username will become available for others to use
  • All messages, photos, and data will be permanently lost
  • Your purchase history and diamond balance cannot be retrieved
  • You will need to create a completely new account to use OnlyDMs again
