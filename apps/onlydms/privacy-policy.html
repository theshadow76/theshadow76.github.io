Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2025

Important: This Privacy Policy explains how OnlyDMs ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account or use our services, we collect:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, display name, profile photo
  • Profile Data: Bio, location, message pricing preferences
  • Authentication Data: Password (encrypted), Google Sign-In credentials
  • Payment Information: In-app purchase transaction data (processed securely through Google Play Billing)

1.2 Communication Data

  • Messages sent and received through the app
  • Audio messages and media files
  • Conversation metadata (timestamps, read status, participant information)
  • Video call logs and durations

1.3 Usage Information

  • Device information (model, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • App usage statistics and analytics
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Push notification tokens

1.4 Media and Files

  • Photos and images you upload to your profile or send in messages
  • Audio recordings for voice messages
  • Files shared through the messaging feature

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

2.1 Service Delivery

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Facilitating messaging and communication between users
  • Processing diamond purchases and message payments
  • Enabling video and voice calling features
  • Providing QR code and NFC profile sharing

2.2 Service Improvement

  • Analyzing usage patterns to improve app functionality
  • Debugging and fixing technical issues
  • Developing new features and services

2.3 Communication

  • Sending push notifications for new messages and calls
  • Providing customer support
  • Sending important service updates and announcements

2.4 Safety and Security

  • Preventing fraud and abuse
  • Enforcing our Terms of Service
  • Protecting user safety and app integrity

3. Data Storage and Security

We take data security seriously and implement industry-standard measures to protect your information:

  • Firebase Security: All data is stored securely using Firebase Cloud Firestore with strict security rules
  • Encryption: Messages and sensitive data are encrypted during transmission using SSL/TLS
  • Authentication: Firebase Authentication ensures secure account access
  • File Storage: Media files are stored in Firebase Storage with access control
  • Payment Security: Financial transactions are processed through Google Play Billing with PCI compliance

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 With Other Users

Your profile information (username, photo, bio, location, message price) is visible to other users of the app to facilitate connections and messaging.

4.2 Service Providers

We share data with trusted third-party service providers:

  • Firebase/Google Cloud: Backend infrastructure and data storage
  • Google Play Services: In-app purchases and billing
  • Push Notification Services: Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, legal process, or government request, or to:

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect our rights and property
  • Prevent fraud or illegal activities
  • Protect the safety of users or the public

4.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity, subject to the same privacy protections.

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access: View and download your personal data
  • Update: Modify your profile information at any time
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object: Opt out of certain data processing activities

5.2 Communication Preferences

  • Control push notification settings in your device or app settings
  • Manage which types of notifications you receive

5.3 Data Portability

You can request a copy of your data in a structured, commonly used format.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services:

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained while your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Most data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion
